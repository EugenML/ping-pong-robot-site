Privacy Policy

Ping Pong Robot · last updated 22 November 2026

What the app collects

Nothing. Ping Pong Robot does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it makes no network requests. The whole game runs on your device.

What stays on your device

The app remembers the robot skill you last chose. That setting is stored only on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Motion

If you turn on the Gyro button, the app reads how you tilt the device to move the view of the table. The motion data is used in the moment, on the device, and is not stored or sent anywhere.
